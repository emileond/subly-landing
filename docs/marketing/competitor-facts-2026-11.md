# Competitor facts sheet (for Subly comparison and alternatives pages)

Researched 5 Nov 2026 with WebSearch and WebFetch. Each claim is followed by its source. "Not stated" means the page I read doesn't mention it. "Unverified" means I couldn't confirm it from a vendor or listing page. Page dates are recorded as shown on the page. A footer copyright year is not a publication date.

Caveats:
- Pages were read through a fetch tool that summarises them. Re-open every price before publishing, and screenshot the pricing pages on the day you publish.
- Capterra, Software Advice and the Cledara help center returned 403 to the fetch tool. Claims that depend on them are marked.

## 1. Rocket Money

**Who it's for:** US consumers. It's a personal budgeting and subscription app. Rocket Money's own pages show no business plan. The "business" link on its homepage goes to Rocket Pro, a separate Rocket Companies mortgage-lending product.
- Source: https://www.rocketmoney.com/ (footer "© 2015-2026"; no page date)
- Source: https://www.rocketmoney.com/learn/personal-finance/how-much-does-rocket-money-cost (published Jan 19, 2026). No business plan is mentioned.

**Regions:** US only. The help article says: "Rocket Money is only available to users located within the United States and with U.S. based banks."
- Source: https://help.rocketmoney.com/articles/79778-does-truebill-support-international-banks (dated September 22, 2026)

**Pricing:**
- **Free plan:** Yes. Includes subscription viewing, spending categories, income vs. spend charts, transaction log, credit score, notifications and balance alerts.
  - Source: https://www.rocketmoney.com/learn/personal-finance/how-much-does-rocket-money-cost (Jan 19, 2026)
- **Premium:** Pay what you think is fair, "typically ranging from $7 to $14 per month, though this range is subject to change." It's billed monthly. The help center says to "Choose a monthly amount that feels right for you" and describes a "paid monthly Premium membership."
  - Source: https://www.rocketmoney.com/learn/personal-finance/how-much-does-rocket-money-cost (Jan 19, 2026)
  - Source: https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/9184529-how-to-sign-up-for-rocket-money-premium (Sept 22, 2026)
- **Premium+:** "a fixed $15/month plus tax," billed monthly. It includes everything in Premium, plus Rowan (AI agent), bill negotiation "with no added success fee," access to apply for the High-Yield Savings Account, and phone support.
  - Source: https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/16802468-upgrading-to-premium (Sept 22, 2026)
  - Source: https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/16802386-introduction-to-premium ("Updated over a week ago")
- **Annual plan:** Not shown on Rocket Money's own pages. Some third-party sites mention annual billing. Unverified.
- **Trial:** "Most new users are eligible for a free trial." Rocket Money doesn't state the length. Third-party sites say 7 days, which I couldn't confirm.
  - Source: https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/9184529-how-to-sign-up-for-rocket-money-premium (Sept 22, 2026)
- **Bill negotiation fee:** "If the negotiation is successful, we'll charge a fee of 35% - 60% of your first year's savings." "As of November 2, 2026," bill negotiation is exclusive to Premium+ with no success fee.
  - Source: https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/9744474-bill-negotiation-charge-explained ("Updated this week")

**How data gets in:** Linked bank accounts through Plaid. Premium adds manual transactions, real-time sync and transaction export.
- Source: https://www.rocketmoney.com/learn/personal-finance/how-much-does-rocket-money-cost (Jan 19, 2026)
- Source: https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/2677184-premium-membership-features ("Updated over a week ago")

**Alerts and channels:**
- Push and email, configurable for Alerts, Tips and Updates.
- Balance alerts: a default alert when checking drops below $200, editable in the mobile app only.
- Rowan works over text message (RCS, US mobile number).
- Sources:
  - https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/934668-updating-your-notification-settings (from search snippet)
  - https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/8418019-how-to-customize-a-low-balance-alert (from search snippet)
  - https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/16760933-what-is-rowan
- No Slack or other business channel is mentioned.

**Team features:** Account sharing "is designed for two people—typically a primary account holder and one partner." "Only one Premium Membership is required." There are no business seats or roles.
- Source: https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/4562634-account-sharing-in-rocket-money (Sept 22, 2026)

**Income and recurring revenue:**
- Personal income vs. spend charts are on the free plan.
- Business recurring revenue (MRR, Stripe) is not stated.
- Source: https://www.rocketmoney.com/learn/personal-finance/how-much-does-rocket-money-cost

**Multi-currency:** Not stated. The service is US-only with US banks (see Regions).

**Cash forecasting:**
- Premium lists payday and calendar views and a bill timeline.
- An explicit cash-flow forecast is not stated, so this is unverified.
- Source: https://www.rocketmoney.com/learn/personal-finance/how-much-does-rocket-money-cost

**AI and agent features:**
- Rowan is "Rocket Money's AI personal finance agent." It is Premium+ only, works over text message, and "may ask you to confirm details before completing certain actions."
  - Source: https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/16760933-what-is-rowan ("Updated today")
  - Source: https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/16802386-introduction-to-premium
- Rowan launched around Sept 2026: https://fintech.global/2026/09/03/rocket-money-launches-ai-agent-to-manage-finances/
- Public API or MCP: not stated.

**Notable limits:**
- US only.
- Sharing is limited to two people.
- Budgets, desktop access, export and cancellation help are Premium only.
- Rowan and bill negotiation are Premium+ only.
- Sources: https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/2677184-premium-membership-features, https://help.rocketmoney.com/en/articles/16802386-introduction-to-premium

## 2. TrackMySubs (trackmysubs.com)

**Who it's for:** "Digital Entrepreneurs, IT Consultants, & Small Businesses." It also highlights agencies, web design and hosting firms, and IT MSPs that manage multiple clients.

- **Enterprise:** $30/month or $299.99/year. Adds multiple users, priority support and unlimited storage.
- **Trial:** No time-limited trial of paid plans is stated.
- Source: https://trackmysubs.com/pricing (footer "© 2022"; no page date). The page labels one tier both "BASIC" and "Unlimited."

**How data gets in:**

- Sources: https://trackmysubs.com, https://trackmysubs.com/frequently-asked-questions/

**Alerts and channels:**

- Sources: https://trackmysubs.com, https://trackmysubs.com/frequently-asked-questions/

**Team features:** "Multiple Users" on Enterprise only. Seat count and roles are not stated.
- Source: https://trackmysubs.com/pricing

**Income and recurring revenue:** Not stated. The product is positioned as expense management.
- Source: https://trackmysubs.com

**Multi-currency:** Yes. "We have over 160 currencies available, including crypto currencies." Each subscription is converted to a preferred currency at a daily rate.
- Source: https://trackmysubs.com/frequently-asked-questions/

**Cash forecasting:** A calendar of upcoming payments ("See what payments are due on what day"). An explicit forecast is not stated.

**AI and API:**

- Source: https://trackmysubs.com

**Regions:** No restriction stated. The company is Chronim Investments Inc.
- Source: https://trackmysubs.com

**Notable limits:**

## 3. SubTracker (subtracker.io)

**Who it's for:** Consumers and households. The tiers are "Perfect for getting started," "For power users," and "For families & small teams." It is run by Innopulse Consulting GmbH, Zug, Switzerland.
- Source: https://subtracker.io (footer "© 2026")

**Pricing (USD):**
- **Free:** "$0" forever. Unlimited subscriptions, CSV import, calendar sync, cost overview and a PWA mobile app.
- **Plus:** $5.90/month or $49.00/year. Adds renewal and price-hike alerts, email reminders, reports and charts, and attachments.
- **Family:** $9.90/month or $79.00/year. Adds up to 10 workspace members, shared tracking and role-based access.
- **Lifetime:** $129 one-time. Access lasts while the service "remains available as an operating service." A year-end offer ends "Dec 31" ("57 days left" on 5 Nov).
- **Trial:** "Start free with 14 days of Plus included," no card required.
- Source: https://subtracker.io/pricing (footer "© 2026")
- Conflict: one of SubTracker's own roundups says the free plan covers up to 10 subscriptions (https://subtracker.io/best/best-subscription-tracker-2026, from search snippet). The pricing page says unlimited. Use the pricing page.

**How data gets in:**
- Manual entry, plus a quick-add library of 180+ services.
- No bank connection: "SubTracker never links to your bank."
- CSV import is listed on the pricing page. The homepage mentions only export ("Export everything as CSV or JSON").
- No inbox scanning is stated.
- Sources: https://subtracker.io, https://subtracker.io/pricing

**Alerts and channels:** Email and in-app alerts, plus calendar sync (Google, Apple, Outlook). Alerts are on Plus and above. No Slack is mentioned.
- Sources: https://subtracker.io, https://subtracker.io/pricing

**Team features:** The Family plan has up to 10 workspace members with role-based access.
- Source: https://subtracker.io/pricing

**Income and recurring revenue:** Not stated.

**Multi-currency:** Yes. "USD, EUR, GBP, CHF, and 20+ more."
- Source: https://subtracker.io/pricing

**Cash forecasting:** Not stated. Current monthly and annual totals are shown.
- Source: https://subtracker.io

**AI and API:** None stated.
- Source: https://subtracker.io

**Regions:** Hosted in the UK under UK GDPR. Market pages exist for the US, CA, UK and AU. No restriction is stated.
- Source: https://subtracker.io

**Notable limits:**
- Manual only, with no bank or inbox import.
- Alerts require a paid plan.
- Built for households, not businesses.

- Source: https://costloop.app (no page date)

- Source: https://costloop.app/pricing (no page date)

- Conflict: CostLoop's Help Center reportedly says the free plan allows 1 subscription, against 5 on the pricing page (search snippet for https://costloop.app/help/). Use the pricing page.

**How data gets in:**
- Manual entry.
- Bank or card CSV upload with recurring-charge detection. No bank connection: "No bank connection required."
- Gmail and Outlook scanning through a Chrome extension, using OAuth with read-only access. "Free users can preview up to 5 detected subscriptions." Pro and Business unlock all results.
- Sources:
  - https://costloop.app
  - https://costloop.app/blog/manage-my-subscriptions-email-scanner/ (published July 15, 2026; updated Aug 12, 2026)
  - https://costloop.app/best-subscription-tracker/ ("Last verified: September 16, 2026")

**Alerts and channels:**

- Sources: https://costloop.app, https://costloop.app/best-subscription-tracker/

**Team features:**
- Business: workspaces and team visibility, with no per-seat charges.
- The AppSumo deal adds team roles and permissions, and approval workflows.
- Sources: https://costloop.app/pricing, https://appsumo.com/products/costloop-e449a275/

**Income and recurring revenue:** Not stated. The product tracks costs only.

**Multi-currency:**
- The homepage says 40+ currencies. The changelog and press page say 113.
- Sources conflict on whether totals convert to a home currency. The homepage and changelog say yes, using daily rates. The Help Center says "does not convert."
- Unverified which is current.
- Sources (search snippets): https://costloop.app, https://costloop.app/changelog/, https://costloop.app/help/

**Cash forecasting:** Not stated. The dashboard shows monthly and annual recurring cost.

**AI and API:** REST API and webhooks on Business. No AI or MCP is stated.

**Regions:** "GDPR compliant" and "EU-based data processing." No restriction is stated.
- Source: https://costloop.app

**Notable limits:**

## 6. Spreadsheets (Google Sheets / Excel templates)

No source is needed for this section.

**Pros:**
- Free or already paid for.
- Fully flexible.
- No vendor lock-in or data sharing.
- Easy to share in Google Sheets.
- Formulas can total monthly and annual cost.

**Cons:**
- Every charge is entered by hand.
- No automatic renewal reminders without scripts or add-ons.
- Price increases go unnoticed until someone checks a statement.
- Currency conversion is manual or formula-based.
- No bank, inbox or Stripe import.
- Copies drift and nobody owns the sheet.
- No audit trail of who changed what.
- Forecasting has to be built and maintained by hand.
- Easy to forget once the person who built it leaves.
